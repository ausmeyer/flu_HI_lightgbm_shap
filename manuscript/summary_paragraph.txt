The main manuscript should now tell a very focused three-element story built around `Table 1` and three figures. First, the Neher/Bedford H3N2 analysis shows that direct sequence-based site-state and substitution models recover the classical H3 antigenic core, with the Neher/Bedford site-state decomposition providing a clear visual summary of the dominant antigenic head positions and with little scientific benefit from adding patristic distance. Second, the Harvey/WIC analysis shows that the most important improvement is passage filtering, not phylogenetic adjustment: excluding egg-passaged, mixed-passaged, and unknown-passage observations yields a filtered Harvey/WIC site-state model that remains predictive while producing the clearest and most biologically interpretable contemporary ranking of antigenically relevant sites. Third, the cross-study concordance figure shows that these primary models do not stand alone: the Neher/Bedford model most sharply recovers the classical canonical signal, whereas the filtered Harvey/WIC model provides the strongest overall consensus with Harvey, Koel, Neher, McWhite, PLoS/Meyer, and independent sequence-only LightGBM+SHAP analyses, including large strictly controlled unpassaged datasets. Across these comparisons, SHAP-based sequence rankings consistently outperform sitewise dN/dS as a guide to likely antigenically important positions. The supplement should then support, rather than complicate, this message by documenting primary-model diagnostics first and framing unfiltered Harvey/WIC and patristic analyses explicitly as sensitivity tests.
