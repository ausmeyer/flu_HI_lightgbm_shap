PNAS main text versus SI plan

Main manuscript
- Keep the story centered on two models only:
  `Neher/Bedford H3N2` and filtered `Harvey/WIC H3N2`
- Keep the main text focused on three questions:
  1. Can direct sequence models recover the classical H3 antigenic core?
  2. Does passage filtering improve interpretability in WIC?
  3. Do the primary models agree with prior literature and independent sequence-only analyses?

Main manuscript should include
- Title
- Significance statement
- Abstract
- Introduction text
- Results
- Discussion
- Materials and Methods
- Acknowledgments
- References

Main-text figures
- Figure 1:
  `Neher/Bedford H3N2` site-component top-30 plot
- Figure 2:
  filtered `Harvey/WIC H3N2` site-component top-30 plot
- Figure 3:
  cross-study concordance summary

Main-text tables
- Table 1:
  dataset/model summary for the three non-patristic analyses with site-state and substitution performance
- No additional main-text tables are currently needed unless drafting reveals a genuine explanatory gap

Main-text results that should stay concise
- Patristic distance:
  one short paragraph in main text as a sensitivity result
- Full WIC model:
  one short paragraph showing why filtering was adopted
- Substitution models:
  mention briefly for interpretability and classical-site recovery, but do not center them

SI Appendix
- Detailed dataset construction and preprocessing
- Alignment and numbering details
- Additional methods details for SHAP, stability, and cross-study comparison
- Full WIC model results
- All patristic model results
- Additional substitution-model figures and tables if needed during drafting or revision
- Full integrated site comparison tables
- Full literature annotation tables
- Extended CV tables
- Extended predicted-versus-actual plots
- Detailed discussion of the WIC two-cluster structure
- Tree-building details and patristic-distance construction

What to avoid in the main manuscript
- Full six-model narrative
- Long methods digressions
- Multiple nearly redundant figure variants
- Large tables of all ranked sites
- Detailed per-study forensic discussion unless it directly sharpens the main conclusion

Main-page-budget mindset
- Introduction:
  about 0.75 to 1 page
- Results:
  about 4 to 5 pages
- Discussion:
  about 1 to 1.5 pages
- Methods:
  about 1 to 1.5 pages
- Figures/tables:
  compact, multi-panel, and high-yield only

Bottom line
- Main text should make one clean claim:
  sequence-based models recover robust H3 antigenic determinants, and the filtered Harvey/WIC site-state model provides the strongest modern consensus signal.
- Everything else should support that claim without crowding it.
