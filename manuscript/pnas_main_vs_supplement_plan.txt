PNAS main text versus supplement plan

Main-text objective
- Use the main manuscript to answer one biological question:
  which H3N2 HA sites are most robustly supported as antigenically informative across HI datasets and prior analytical frameworks?
- Everything in the main text should serve that question directly.

Main-text narrative
- Primary benchmark analysis:
  `Neher/Bedford H3N2`
- Primary modern consensus analysis:
  filtered `Harvey/WIC H3N2`
- Supporting synthesis:
  cross-study concordance across benchmark site sets, passaging analyses, structural-evolution work, and independent sequence-only ML analyses

Main-text structure
- Opening introduction text
- Results
- Discussion
- Materials and Methods

Main-text graphical elements
- Table 1:
  overview of the three non-patristic analyses and site-state/substitution performance
- Figure 1:
  Neher/Bedford H3N2 site-component top 30
- Figure 2:
  filtered Harvey/WIC H3N2 site-component top 30
- Figure 3:
  cross-study concordance

Main-text material that should stay in the main manuscript
- Why antigenic-site prioritization matters biologically and for surveillance
- Why Neher/Bedford H3N2 is the right benchmark dataset
- Why Harvey/WIC is the right modern large-scale dataset
- Why passage filtering matters more than patristic adjustment
- Why SHAP-based sequence rankings are more useful than dN/dS for this problem
- The distinction between:
  the sharpest benchmark recovery,
  and the broadest modern consensus signal

Main-text material that should be brief
- Full WIC analysis without filtering
- Patristic-distance analyses
- Substitution models
- Detailed cross-validation mechanics
- Extended discussion of the WIC two-cluster phenomenon

Supplement objective
- Provide enough detail that the main claims are transparent, reproducible, and well-defended without distracting from the main paper.

Current supplement contents that support the main story
- S1-S4:
  primary Neher/Bedford H3N2 diagnostics
- S5-S9:
  primary filtered Harvey/WIC H3N2 diagnostics
- S10-S15:
  sensitivity analyses for unfiltered WIC and patristic variants
- Table S1:
  broader model-performance summary
- Table S2:
  compact reference-overlap summary

What should remain in the supplement by default
- Full model-grid results
- Held-out comparisons
- Predicted-versus-actual plots
- Stability analyses
- Residualization diagnostics
- Neher-style grouped holdout analog figures
- Unfiltered Harvey/WIC sensitivity figures
- Patristic sensitivity figures
- Expanded methodological detail
- Extended literature comparison tables

What may later be added to the supplement if drafting or review requires it
- substitution-model top-site figures for the two primary datasets
- expanded Neher supplementary-site benchmark analysis
- additional rank-correlation or overlap panels
- glycosylation-oriented post hoc analyses if they become part of the manuscript argument

Page-budget plan for PNAS
- Aim for:
  about 10 to 12 pages total
- Use the extra space for:
  a stronger introduction,
  a more developed discussion,
  and a clear cross-study synthesis
- Do not spend that space on:
  extensive methods detail or a full tour of the model grid

Writing rule
- The main paper should read as a study about antigenic interpretation.
- The supplement should read as the evidentiary support structure for that interpretation.
